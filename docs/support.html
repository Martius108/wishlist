Common questions

Where are my wishes stored?
Wishes and settings are stored locally on your device. If iCloud is available and enabled for the app, they may sync through your private iCloud account.

How do the three lists differ?
They let you organize wishes by your own short-term, mid-term, and long-term plans. Moving between the lists does not change a wish automatically.

Does Wishlist open saved links automatically?
No. Links are stored with the wish and shared only when you use the system share action.

Does the app require a separate account?
No. Wishlist does not require an account from the developer.
