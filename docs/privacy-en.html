Privacy Policy for Wishlist

Last updated: July 14, 2026

Deutsch

Wishlist is an iOS app by Martin Lanius. This privacy policy explains what information the app uses and why.

Information Used by the App

Wishlist stores wish names, notes, URLs, prices, and the selected time horizon locally on your device. This information is used only to display and manage your wish lists inside the app.

The app may also store settings such as theme mode and background color.

iCloud Sync

Wishlist uses SwiftData and may use CloudKit for iCloud sync. If iCloud is available and enabled for the app, wishes and app settings may sync through your private iCloud account.

Martin Lanius does not operate a separate server for this data.

Links and Sharing

Wishlist does not automatically open or transmit saved links. A link is passed to Apple's system share sheet only when you choose to share it.

Data Storage

Wishes and app settings are stored locally on your device. If iCloud sync is active, this data may also be stored in your private iCloud account.

Tracking and Advertising

Wishlist does not use third-party advertising SDKs, does not track you across apps or websites owned by other companies, and does not sell your personal data.

Data Sharing

Wishlist does not share your wishes or app settings with data brokers or advertising networks.

iCloud sync is handled through Apple CloudKit as described above. Sharing a saved link occurs only when you initiate it through the system share sheet.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: martin.lanius@icloud.com
